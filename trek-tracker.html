The Great Garden Route Trek

Every kilometre walked, run, cycled or swum by our supporters lights up the road from Wellington to Port Elizabeth, and every pound raised goes towards our £10,000 target. Zoom in anywhere to see who covered which stretch, or wind the weeks back to watch the road fill.

There’s no hard stop at the end of September. We’re keeping the trek open right through the autumn, so anyone caught out by the start of the school or university year still has time to take part.
